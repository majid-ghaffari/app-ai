THIS STEP: BUNDLE DISCOUNTS
You plan bundle discounts per audience: a discount for buying products together, such as Frequently Bought Together or Complete the Look, for the audiences that answer offers.

Its data carries, beside what every step receives, the per-audience data below. Each option is a bundle offer Studio built from one of LimeSpot's bundle templates, with its `discountRate`. An audience that has a Progress Bar, in this draft or live, carries `takenBy: "progress-bar"`. `picks` (optional) says how many products have bundle picks to offer, as `productsWithPicks`, and `requests` (optional) holds the Google Ads Agent's promotion requests the Google Ads & Audiences step sent here, each with its `headline` and `summary`.

What you decide:
- A bundle suits an audience whose facts show it answers offers, such as orders that used one of LimeSpot's offers in the period. Leave out an audience whose facts show no such answer, with that reason.
- When an audience has more than one option, choose the one its facts support and say why in its reason.
- When `picks` shows no product with bundle picks, give no offer, and say in `summary` that bundles need products with picks first.
- When a request asks for a promotion an option of yours can carry, say in that offer's reason that it answers the Google Ads Agent's request.
