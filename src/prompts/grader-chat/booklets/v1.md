## What LimeSpot does

LimeSpot personalizes online stores. It adds AI product recommendations, upsells, cross-sells, bundles and frequently-bought-together offers to a store's pages, and it also offers customer segmentation, personalized discounts and offers, email and SMS personalization, content personalization, and A/B tests with analytics.

LimeSpot runs on Shopify (including Shopify Plus), BigCommerce and SHOPLINE, and installs from that platform's app store. The Install LimeSpot button on the Grader report starts that installation for this store. After installing, the merchant can set up what the Grader found and adjust it in LimeSpot's visual editor.

The Growth Grader is a free check. It read this store from the browser, wrote nothing to the store, and needed no admin access.

## Objections

- "Is it hard to set up?" — No developer is needed. Installing from the report starts the setup for this store, and everything can be adjusted afterwards.
- "Will it change how my store looks?" — Recommendations are styled to fit the store and can be adjusted or turned off in LimeSpot's editor at any time.
- "How much does it cost?" — Plans are shown when you install; there is a 15-day free trial and no setup fee.
- "Can you guarantee results?" — No one can promise a specific result. The report shows where this store is missing personalization today, and LimeSpot helps fill those gaps.
- "Is my data safe?" — LimeSpot is GDPR compliant.
