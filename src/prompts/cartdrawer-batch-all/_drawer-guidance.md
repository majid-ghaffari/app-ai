## Cart-drawer guidance (the surface you are planning)

The cart drawer is a COMPACT slide-out OVERLAY: it holds the cart's line items + a subtotal + a checkout CTA, in a NARROW column. Everything you place must respect that narrow, short space.

- **The canonical spot** is a "shoppers also bought" / cross-sell / upsell strip placed BELOW the line items and ABOVE the checkout CTA — so it never pushes the checkout button out of reach. The shopper is mid-checkout; a well-placed strip nudges one more item into the order without getting in the way of finishing.
- **It must be a SMALL box that FITS the drawer's narrow width.** Favor a compact horizontal / scroll strip or a 1–2 item layout; do NOT overwhelm a narrow drawer with a big multi-row grid. Prefer few items (a short carousel / row), square-or-small images, and no giant arrows.
- **Box fit for a drawer.** Cross-sell / bought-together / you-may-like / upsell boxes are the STRONG fits for a cart drawer — the shopper is mid-checkout, so complementary and add-on recommendations pay off. `RecentViews` (Recently Viewed) is a WEAKER fit inside a drawer; prefer it only if nothing better fits.
- **Usually ONE box is right for a compact drawer** — occasionally two. Quality over quantity: a cramped drawer is worse than none. If no candidate lets a box fit cleanly, place nothing rather than crowd the drawer.
- **Evidence** = a screenshot of the OPEN drawer at desktop + mobile widths (per the mobile-first rule) + the numbered candidate manifest (INSERT anchors + any REPLACE grids inside the drawer).
