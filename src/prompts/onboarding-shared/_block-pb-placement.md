**No styling and no placement for the bar** — the bar's look is set by its campaign template (Shopify Free Shipping, etc.), NOT by an appearance patch, and its position is FIXED: it always renders at the very TOP of the Cart page, so you do NOT choose where it sits. You decide ONLY WHETHER the store should have one; when it fits, emit a one-sentence `reasoning` and nothing else. There is NO `position`, `anchorNumber`, `appearancePatch`, `appearancePatchMobile`, or `styleReferenceSelector` on the progress bar.
